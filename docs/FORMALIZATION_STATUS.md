## Boundaries

The canonical-block calculations are proved in [`KroneckerSum`](../FD56/Hilbert/KroneckerSum.lean): a finite direct sum has bounded-kernel dimension ∑ max(0, d + 1 − e), and these dimensions determine its index list up to permutation. [`BlockSum`](../FD56/Hilbert/BlockSum.lean) proves additivity and that square polynomial blocks with nonzero determinant contribute no kernel. [`ConstantEquivalence`](../FD56/Hilbert/ConstantEquivalence.lean) proves invariance under constant invertible coordinate changes satisfying the intertwining equations. Actual minimal kernel degrees are computed for the families listed above. Existence of a full canonical decomposition and its regular Jordan blocks remain separate.

`TraceZeroStabilizer` is a subspace of the full `SLRadical`; they are not equated. The full SL equations include the scalar term cI.

`slHilbert` is the dimension of actual bounded polynomial radical vectors. Generic Hilbert functions and actual minimal kernel degrees are proved in the regimes listed above, including the full `(11k,8k)` family. For the remaining proposed spectra, `slHilbert_of_polynomial_basis` provides a criterion with explicit matrix rank and degree hypotheses; verifying those hypotheses and identifying the full Jordan–Kronecker blocks remain open here.

`glFormula` and `slFormula` implement the candidate nondivisible-parameter formulas. Their intended domain is positive n and m with 0 < n % m < m. Lean's total natural-number division also gives values outside that domain; those values have no asserted classification meaning. In particular, these functions do not implement the divisible Jordan cases.

## Remaining classification proofs

1. For nondivisible n ≥ 2m, construct the generic auxiliary polynomial bases with the required recurrences and balanced degrees. These constructions and the full GL kernel degree formula are proved for m < n < 2m. The general structured formula follows from explicit recurrence, degree, pointwise-rank, leading-rank, and nullity tests; establishing the remaining tests at greater block depth remains.
2. Rank-open loci, codimension estimates, and genericity on projective lines.
3. The Hirzebruch-surface bundle argument and the required application of the Coskun–Huizenga theorem.
4. The generic SL Hilbert maximum formula in the remaining parameters. The full `(11k,8k)` family and n = m + 1 are proved, as are the stated high-degree values throughout m < n < 2m.
5. Jordan blocks in the remaining parameter regimes and the complete generic GL/SL classification. The actual generic GL/SL Hilbert functions throughout m ≥ n are proved; existence and identification of a full canonical decomposition remain separate.

These gaps are not assumed as axioms. Passing the build certifies the included Lean statements, not the complete manuscript or its novelty.
