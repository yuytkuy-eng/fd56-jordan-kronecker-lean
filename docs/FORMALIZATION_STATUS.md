## Boundaries

The canonical-block calculations are proved in [`KroneckerSum`](../FD56/Hilbert/KroneckerSum.lean): a finite direct sum has bounded-kernel dimension ∑ max(0, d + 1 − e), and these dimensions determine its index list up to permutation. [`BlockSum`](../FD56/Hilbert/BlockSum.lean) proves additivity and that square polynomial blocks with nonzero determinant contribute no kernel. [`ConstantEquivalence`](../FD56/Hilbert/ConstantEquivalence.lean) proves invariance under constant invertible coordinate changes satisfying the intertwining equations. These results do not assert existence of a canonical decomposition for an arbitrary pencil or identify the generic GL/SL index lists.

`TraceZeroStabilizer` is a subspace of the full `SLRadical`; they are not equated. The full SL equations include the scalar term cI.

`slHilbert` is the dimension of actual bounded polynomial radical vectors. `slHilbert_of_polynomial_basis` now derives `HasHilbertProfile` from explicit matrix rank and degree hypotheses. Verification of those hypotheses for the proposed generic spectra, and the identification with Jordan–Kronecker blocks, remain open here.

`glFormula` and `slFormula` implement the candidate nondivisible-parameter formulas. Their intended domain is positive n and m with 0 < n % m < m. Lean's total natural-number division also gives values outside that domain; those values have no asserted classification meaning. In particular, these functions do not implement the divisible Jordan cases.

## Remaining classification proofs

1. For n > m, construct the generic auxiliary polynomial bases with the required ranks and balanced degrees. The structured GL Hilbert formula follows from explicit recurrence, degree, pointwise-rank, leading-rank, and nullity tests; the auxiliary degree bounds are derived from the recurrence and leading observability rows. The generic nullity, the observability ranks at infinity, and full row rank of P at every finite parameter are proved. Establishing the remaining tests for the generic family and identifying the Jordan–Kronecker blocks remain.
2. Rank-open loci, codimension estimates, and genericity on projective lines.
3. The Hirzebruch-surface bundle argument and the required application of the Coskun–Huizenga theorem.
4. Realization of generic SL extensions and the geometric Hilbert maximum formula.
5. Jordan blocks in the remaining parameter regimes and the complete generic GL/SL classification. The actual generic GL/SL Hilbert functions throughout m > n are proved; existence and identification of a full canonical decomposition remain separate.

These gaps are not assumed as axioms. Passing the build certifies the included Lean statements, not the complete manuscript or its novelty.
